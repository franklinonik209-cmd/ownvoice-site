Your first message

Three lines are enough to start.

  • Your book title, and whether the manuscript is finished.
  • Its approximate length, in words or chapters, so we can work out the finished hours.
  • Anything we should know about names, places, or terms in the book that carry a particular pronunciation.

We will reply with the next steps and a fixed quote in writing, at the published rate: $200 per finished hour plus a $400 production fee, $1,400 minimum, $2,900 maximum.

For a heritage project under the Ghost Narrator service line, tell us about the work and the family or history it carries, and we will reply with how the service line fits and a quote in writing.
