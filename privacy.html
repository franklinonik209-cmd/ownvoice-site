Privacy policy

How OwnVoice Studio handles your information, your manuscript, and your voice, stated plainly.

OwnVoice Studio is a product of Onikan Global Solutions LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy covers this website and the OwnVoice Studio audiobook production service, including the Ghost Narrator service line for heritage audiobooks.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

The short version

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. We collect only what you choose to send us to discuss or produce your book. Your voice is cloned only after you record it yourself and tick the consent attestations yourself. We never sell your information, your manuscript, or your voice, and your cloned voice is never used for another author's book.

This website

This is a static website. It has no account system, no contact form that stores submissions on this site, and no embedded third party trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site's own domain. When you write to us, you do so from your own email program or phone, and that message travels through your provider to ours.

Information you send us

When you contact the studio or start a project, you may send us:

  • Your name and contact details, so we can reply and run your project
  • Your manuscript and notes about your book, including pronunciation guidance
  • A recording of your own voice, made by you in the intake flow
  • Your consent attestations, ticked by you as part of intake
  • Your chapter review notes and correction requests

We use this material for one purpose: to respond to you and to produce your audiobook. We do not use your manuscript to train general purpose models for other customers, and we do not publish, excerpt, or share your book on our own behalf.

Your voice and its clone

Voice handling is consent first, by design:

  • We clone only the author's own voice, recorded by the author.
  • A clone is built only after the author has read and ticked the consent attestations in the intake flow. Without them, no clone is built.
  • The clone is built for your book and used for your project. It is not sold, not shared, and not used to narrate another author's work.
  • Narration produced from your clone is disclosed as digitally produced narration, as described in our Terms.

You may ask us at any time, by email, what voice material of yours we hold and ask us to delete it. Deleting the underlying voice material does not change masters already delivered to you, which are yours.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your manuscript, recordings, or contact details with advertisers or data brokers. Material moves only where your project requires it: to you, on delivery, or where the law requires disclosure.

Retention and deletion

We keep project material while your project is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can answer questions about delivered work and meet our record keeping obligations. You may request deletion of your manuscript copies and voice recordings at any time by writing to contact@onikanglobal.com, and we will confirm deletion by reply. Delivered masters are your property and are not affected by a deletion request on our side.

Security

Access to manuscripts and voice recordings is limited to the people working on your production. No system is perfect, and we encourage authors to keep their own copies of their manuscripts and delivered masters in a place they trust.

Children

OwnVoice Studio is a production service for authors and creators. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly accept voice recordings from children for cloning.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change applies to new projects. For a project already in production, we will tell the author directly if a change affects their material.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request, go to Onikan Global Solutions LLC at contact@onikanglobal.com or (917) 727-6096. We are based in Pennsylvania.
