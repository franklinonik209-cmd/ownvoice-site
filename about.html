About the studio

OwnVoice Studio is the audiobook studio of Onikan Global Solutions LLC, a Pennsylvania company that builds publishing, media, and software with one rule over all of it: the work belongs to the person it was made for.

Why we built it

A book should sound like its author.

Readers form a bond with an author's voice long before they hear it out loud. They know how the author thinks, jokes, warns, and comforts, because the writing carried all of that. An audiobook should keep that bond, not hand it to a stranger.

Recording a full book by hand is days of studio work, and most authors are writers, not performers with a studio at home. So their books stay silent in audio, or arrive in a voice that is not theirs.

OwnVoice Studio exists to close that gap with care: the author's own voice, cloned only with the author's own consent, produced chapter by chapter, reviewed for pronunciation with the author, and delivered as masters the author owns outright.

House rules

How this studio works, on every project

Consent is a step, not a footnote

Voice intake includes consent attestations the author reads and ticks personally. No attestations, no clone. We do not take consent on anyone's behalf.

Ownership is the point

Finished masters are owned outright by the author. That is written into how the studio sells its work, and it is stated on this site in the open.

Prices are published

One rate, one fee, a floor and a ceiling, printed on the Pricing page for anyone to check before they write to us.

The method is disclosed

Narration is digitally produced from the author's cloned voice. We say so plainly, because a listener deserves to know and an author deserves a studio that is honest about its craft.

Pronunciation is reviewed with you

Names, places, and terms are checked with the author in the review loop. The book is the author's; the last word on how it sounds is the author's too.

One company stands behind it

OwnVoice Studio is a product of Onikan Global Solutions LLC, based in Pennsylvania. The company, its contact details, and its terms are on this site, in full.
