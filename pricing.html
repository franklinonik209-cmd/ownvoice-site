Pricing

The whole pricing model fits in one line, and it is the same for every author: $200 per finished hour plus a $400 production fee, $1,400 minimum, $2,900 maximum.

The rate card

How your price is worked out

Finished hours means the final running time of the delivered audiobook. Your manuscript length and reading pace set the finished hours, and we confirm the exact figure, and the exact price, in your written quote before production begins.

Finished hoursHow it is calculatedYour price
5 hours 5 times $200 is $1,000, plus the $400 fee is $1,400 $1,400, the minimum
7 hours 7 times $200 is $1,400, plus the $400 fee $1,800, the typical book
10 hours 10 times $200 is $2,000, plus the $400 fee $2,400
12.5 hours and longer The rate reaches $2,900 at 12.5 hours, and the maximum holds from there $2,900, the maximum

Books shorter than five finished hours are produced at the $1,400 minimum. Books longer than 12.5 finished hours are produced at the $2,900 maximum. Between the floor and the ceiling, the rate and the fee are the whole price.

Included in every production

  • Consent first voice intake, in your own voice
  • Your cloned voice, built for your book
  • Full narration production, chapter by chapter
  • The pronunciation review loop, with you
  • Chapter review and corrections
  • Finished masters, owned outright by you

What we will never do with price

  • No quote that grows after production begins
  • No per revision charges inside the review loop
  • No rental or license fee for your own masters
  • No charge above the $2,900 maximum, for any book

Your written quote is the price. If we have it in writing, that is what your book costs.
