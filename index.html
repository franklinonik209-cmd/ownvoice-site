The studio

An audiobook in the voice your readers already trust: yours.

Most authors face a hard choice. Record the audiobook yourself and spend days in front of a microphone, or hire a studio narrator and let a stranger carry your book. OwnVoice Studio is the third way.

You record a voice sample in your own voice and confirm your consent. We build a clone of your voice for your book, produce the narration chapter by chapter, and work through pronunciation with you until names and terms sound right. You review the chapters, and we deliver finished masters that belong to you.

The narration is digitally produced from your own voice, and we say so plainly, on this page and in our terms. Honesty about the method is part of the product.

Consent and ethics

Your voice is yours. We work on that basis, in writing.

Voice cloning asks for trust, so the rules are stated before any recording happens, not after.

Only your own voice

We clone the voice of the author, recorded by the author. We do not clone the voice of any other person, and we do not build a voice from recordings you do not have the right to use.

Consent you give yourself

Intake includes consent attestations that you read and tick yourself, in the studio flow, before your voice is built. No consent, no clone. That is the rule, without exception.

Built for your book

Your cloned voice is built for your project and used for your project. It is not sold, not shared, and not used to narrate another author's book.

Disclosed, always

The finished audiobook is narrated by a digital clone of your voice, produced with your consent. We disclose that plainly, and we encourage you to disclose it wherever your audiobook is offered.
